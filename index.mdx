## Why Buzz Health

- **Live pricing across nearby pharmacies.** Search by ZIP code, by coordinates, or for the specific pharmacies you choose.
- **One consistent API.** Nine pricing endpoints share one request shape and one response envelope, so you learn it once.
- **Built for healthcare.** Buzz Health is HITRUST i1 certified and has completed a SOC 2 Type II examination [APPROVE V-022].

Buzz Health connects the prescription ecosystem, from pharmacies to the apps people use to manage their prescriptions.
