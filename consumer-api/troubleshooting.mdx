Solutions to the issues integrators run into most often. For the error format and status codes, see [Errors](/consumer-api/errors).

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="The drug dictionary comes back empty">

Send the drug's brand name in `drugParameters.bn` along with `"includeDrugDictionary": true`. Without `bn`, the dictionary may be empty for many drugs [VERIFY V-007].

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I get a 404 for a drug I know exists">

A `404` can mean that no pharmacy within your search area has a price for that drug and quantity. Try a larger `radius` (up to 100 miles), a different location, or a common quantity from the drug dictionary. If you priced by NDC, confirm it's 11 digits without hyphens.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A pharmacy was priced for a different NDC">

With `predictiveNDCs` on (the default), a pharmacy may be priced for an equivalent NDC. Compare `ndcSelected` in each result with `ndcRepresented` in `pricedDrugInfo`. Set `predictiveNDCs` to `false` to price only the NDC you sent [VERIFY V-035].

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Requests return 401 after working for a while">

Access tokens expire after 24 hours. Cache the token with its expiry time, request a new one shortly before it expires, and retry once after any `401`.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="One endpoint returns 403 while others work">

Endpoints are enabled for each client individually. Ask your account team to enable the endpoint for your client [TBD V-017].

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Browser requests fail with a CORS error">

The Consumer API is built for server-to-server calls. Send requests from your server and pass the results to your app. This also keeps your credentials out of the browser.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Results include fewer pharmacies than expected">

Results reflect your program's pharmacy network configuration and the search radius. Widen the `radius`, or ask your account team about your configuration.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sorting by price gives odd results">

Prices and other numbers are returned as strings. Convert `price` to a number before sorting, or `"100.00"` will sort before `"20.00"`.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
