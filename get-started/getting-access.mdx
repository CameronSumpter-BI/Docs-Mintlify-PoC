Access to the Consumer API starts with your Buzz Health account team. This page describes the general path from first credentials to production [APPROVE V-015].

<Steps>

<Step title="Talk to your account team">

Your account team sets up your client and confirms which pricing endpoints your integration needs. To get started, contact Buzz Health [TBD V-017].

</Step>

<Step title="Receive test credentials">

You receive a client ID, a user ID, and a user secret for a non-production environment, together with its base URL. If your program uses a group number, you receive that too. Test environments return synthetic pricing, not real prices.

</Step>

<Step title="Build and test your integration">

Follow the [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart), then build out the requests your app needs. Endpoints are enabled for your client individually. If an endpoint returns `403`, ask your account team to enable it.

</Step>

<Step title="Go live">

When testing is complete, you receive production credentials and a production base URL. Switch your configuration, and your integration is live.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Keep credentials safe

- Store your user secret on your server, in a secrets manager or environment variable. Never ship it in a web page or mobile app.
- Use separate credentials for each environment.
- If you think a secret has been exposed, contact your account team to replace it [TBD V-017].

<Tip>

**Tell us your expected volume.** Share your anticipated request throughput with your account team before you go live.

</Tip>
